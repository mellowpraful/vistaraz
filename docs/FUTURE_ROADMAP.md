# CrisisOS — Product Roadmap & Future Horizons

This document outlines the strategic product roadmap for future iterations of CrisisOS.

---

## 1. Phase 2: Autonomous Edge & Drone Integration (Q3 2026)
- **Autonomous UAV Reconnaissance:** Direct API integration with DJI & Skydio enterprise drones for automated aerial damage assessment.
- **Computer Vision Triage:** Real-time aerial flood boundary segmentation and structural damage severity scoring.

## 2. Phase 3: Satellite SAR & Earth Observation (Q4 2026)
- **Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR):** Automated ingestion of Sentinel-1 and NISAR satellite radar passes to map night-time water inundation through cloud cover.
- **Wildfire Thermal Infrared Tracking:** Modis / VIIRS hotspot integration for automated fire perimeter expansion alerts.

## 3. Phase 4: Offline P2P Mesh & Satellite Communications (Q1 2027)
- **Zero-Infrastructure Mesh Network:** Bluetooth LE / LoRa peer-to-peer relay for field teams operating in cellular blackout areas.
- **Starlink Direct-to-Cell Handoff:** Guaranteed satellite fallback for high-priority emergency command telemetry.
